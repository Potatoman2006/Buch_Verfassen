# World review before drafting

The worldbuilding gate was completed before prose drafting on 5 September 2026.

The active setting contains 23 source modules, 30,220 words of lore, twelve detailed regional powers, eight peoples, six magical crafts, and a wider inhabited horizon. Its 121 internal links, dependency graph, map references, and generated-reader source consistency passed validation. An independent editorial read covered all modules rather than treating those mechanical checks as proof of literary depth.

## Findings resolved before the story

- The old administrative and financial-record emphasis was replaced with cultural life, faith, dynastic conflict, supernatural danger, and historical consequences.
- A first pass was too cautious and practical. The world was strengthened with the Mourning Host, the Thorn Court and Briar-Wake, White Keel, knell-hounds, distinct dramatic workings, and inhabited outer regions.
- The historical chronology, differing lifespans, local route directions, and regional religious names were reconciled.
- Regional map placements were corrected to match painted land and islands. Obsolete wider-map names were replaced with the actual places described in the new lore.
- Vampirism fixes turning, feeding, freshness, starvation, sunlight, air, injury, death, and the absence of sire control. Blood from vampires cannot provide a closed feeding loop. An isolated sibling cannot sustain another person indefinitely.
- Yseld has pre-existing but fragile coerced access to blood. Creating a second dependent vampire worsens that shortage. Her knowledge and coercion provide power without a supernatural obedience rule.
- The Rookharbour corridor has three consistent routes, seasons, occupied daytime shelters, a working community, and a local ancient site with limited physical reach.

## Decision and limits

The reviewed world is sufficient for this bounded novella. This is a substantial first edition, not a claim to equal decades of accumulated franchise lore. Additional outer-country modules and town maps remain possible without reopening this story's foundations.

The story's exact eleven-module source packet and hashes are preserved in `world_packet/`. The story spans autumn 250 through autumn 255 IS; those events belong to its local continuity. It may introduce ordinary local details but may not invent a new magical rescue, reverse established history, or silently change the 250 IS world baseline.
